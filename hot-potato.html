How To Play
💣
Real Bomb
A glowing orange bomb bounces between players. Pass it before the fuse hits zero — or BOOM.
🔵
Fake Bomb
Sometimes a blue bomb appears. DO NOT pass it — hold it until the round ends. Passing a fake loses a life.
❤️
Lives
Each player starts with 5 lives. Explode or wrongly pass a fake = lose a life. Zero lives = eliminated.
⚡
Fuse Shrinks
Every round the fuse gets shorter. Round 10 is a heart attack.
🏆
Win
Survive all 10 rounds. The loser gets roasted.
⌨️
2P Controls
Player 1: press A or tap left zone. Player 2: press L or tap right zone.
👆
1P Controls
Tap PASS button to send the bomb back. Hold during fake bombs.
